# Whack-a-Weirdo — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 29, 2026
**Developer and data controller:** Robert Vandal, operating independently as Buffster in Québec, Canada
**Privacy contact:** robert195505@pm.me

## About Whack-a-Weirdo

Whack-a-Weirdo is a single-player Meta Quest game playable in virtual reality (VR) and mixed reality (MR), designed for seated play and intended for players age 10 and older. The app is self-certified with Meta as Mixed Ages. It supports offline play and local scores. Optional worldwide leaderboards and Meta account/age-group features are planned; they are not enabled for general users while authorization, integration, and testing are completed.

## Information the game handles

- **Local gameplay data:** The game may save local player nicknames, best scores, game and difficulty choices (including VR or MR), and comfort/settings preferences on the headset. Optional developer-enabled performance diagnostics may record timing and device-performance details locally during testing.
- **Meta account and worldwide leaderboards (when enabled):** To provide optional worldwide rankings, the game uses Meta Horizon Platform services to process the authorized player's Meta User ID, permitted Horizon username, leaderboard ranking, and eligible completed three-round score. The six worldwide categories are VR Easy, Normal, and Hard, and MR Easy, Normal, and Hard. Local player nicknames are not uploaded to Meta's leaderboards. Profile photos are not used.
- **Meta user age group (when enabled):** The game requests Meta's age-group category to decide whether optional online leaderboard access is appropriate. It does not ask for a birth date or exact age, and age-group information is not used for advertising, marketing, or profiling.
- **Headset and MR information:** Meta Quest processes headset/controller tracking necessary for gameplay and passthrough imagery for MR. Whack-a-Weirdo does not intentionally record, retain, or transmit passthrough camera images, room video, or microphone recordings to the developer.
- **Support and privacy requests:** If you email the developer, your address and the information you voluntarily provide are used to reply and handle your request. Email delivery uses the developer's email service; please do not send passwords, account secrets, dates of birth, or other sensitive credentials.

## Why information is used

Local information is used to run the game, keep settings and personal bests, and display local results. When authorized online features become available, Meta account and leaderboard information will be used only to identify the eligible player for those optional functions, submit eligible completed-game scores, and show permitted worldwide rankings. Age-group information is used to restrict online features appropriately. The game does not use third-party advertising or behavioral profiling.

## Children and age-appropriate controls

The app is intended for mixed ages, including players ages 10–12 and 13+, where permitted by Meta's regional rules, and is not intended for children younger than 10. When the age-group integration is enabled, players Meta identifies in a child age category, or whose online eligibility is unknown, unavailable, or denied, will use local-only scores and gameplay. Teen and adult access to optional worldwide leaderboards will still require successful Meta authorization and applicable account permissions. All players can continue the offline game without online leaderboards.

## Sharing and service providers

Meta provides the Quest platform, Meta Horizon account services, and optional worldwide leaderboard infrastructure. When those features are used, relevant account identifiers, permitted usernames, scores, and rankings are processed through Meta's services under Meta's own terms and privacy practices. Buffster does not operate an independent online player-account or leaderboard server, does not sell player data, and does not disclose game-derived Meta user data to third-party advertising or analytics providers. Support emails are handled through the developer's email service.

## Storage and retention

Local game records remain on the headset until removed using the headset's app-data controls or by uninstalling the game, subject to any Meta device backup/restore features. The developer does not maintain a separate server copy of local gameplay records. If worldwide leaderboards are enabled, Meta controls storage and retention of the data held by its platform service; the game processes only what is needed to provide the authorized feature. The game is designed not to build a separate permanent database of players' age categories. Support emails are kept only as needed to address requests and necessary records, then removed when no longer required.

## Your choices and deletion requests

You can enjoy the offline game without using optional worldwide leaderboards. You can remove local game information through Quest app-data management or uninstalling the app; this may erase saved scores and preferences. To ask what information the developer holds about you, request correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern, email **robert195505@pm.me** with the subject **“Whack-a-Weirdo privacy request”**. Deletion requests are free. We may ask for minimal information needed to identify the relevant records, but never for a password. We will delete data we control where applicable and explain any justified limitation. If the requested information is stored by Meta rather than by Buffster, we will point you to Meta's relevant account/privacy controls and assist where technically possible.

## Security and updates

The game is designed to minimize data sent outside the headset and use Meta's authorized platform interfaces for optional online functionality. This policy will be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The current policy will remain accessible at the URL supplied in the Meta Developer Dashboard.
